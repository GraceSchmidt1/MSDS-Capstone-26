How to read this

Typical rate for its reach
Posts with fewer impressions usually have higher engagement rates. Every post is compared with the usual rate for a post of its reach on the same platform, so a choice isn't credited or blamed for reach.
Lift
Rate minus the typical rate for its reach, in percentage points (pts).
Difference vs. baseline
How much higher or lower the lift is for posts with the choice. Formats are compared with Image; weekend with weekday; 11am or later with before 11am; boosted with not boosted; pillars and segments with posts without them.
Do more / Do less
The whole factor (e.g. format) makes a difference, the choice clearly differs from its baseline after correcting for the number of comparisons, and this holds however reach is modelled.
Promising / Watch
Leaning one way, but not yet conclusive. Keep testing.
No clear difference
Choose on strategy; the data doesn't show it changing engagement.
Not enough posts yet
Fewer than 10 posts with the choice (or its baseline).
Posts needed
Roughly how many more posts with the choice would settle it, if the current difference is real (80% power). '200+' means too small to settle soon.
Caveat
These are associations in past posts, not proof of cause. Other choices made on the same posts are not held fixed.
